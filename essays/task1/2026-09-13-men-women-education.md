## Đề bài

The chart below shows the number of men and women in further education in Britain in three periods and whether they were studying full-time or part-time.

Summarise the information by selecting and reporting the main features, and make comparisons where relevant.

Write at least 150 words.

**Nguồn:** IELTS Academic Writing Sample Tasks — [ielts.org](https://ielts.org/cdn/Sample-tests/ielts-academic-writing-sample-tasks-2023.pdf), Sample Task 1A.

**Mô tả biểu đồ:** Bar chart. Trục tung: "Men and women in further education (thousands)", từ 0 đến 1200. Trục hoành chia 2 nhóm Male và Female, mỗi nhóm có 3 mốc thời gian: 1970/71, 1980/81, 1990/91. Mỗi mốc có 2 cột: cột xám = Part-time education, cột đen = Full-time education.

Số liệu (nghìn người):

| | 1970/71 | 1980/81 | 1990/91 |
|---|---|---|---|
| Male – Part-time | 1000 | 870 | 910 |
| Male – Full-time | 110 | 160 | 260 |
| Female – Part-time | 750 | 830 | 1100 |
| Female – Full-time | 65 | 225 | 260 |
